# IELTS Writing Band Descriptors (Public Version)

Four criteria, each scored 0–9. Final band = average, rounded to nearest 0.5.

Task 1 uses "Task Achievement", Task 2 uses "Task Response" — same concept, slightly different focus.

---

## 1. Task Achievement / Task Response (25%)

Does the response address all parts of the prompt fully and appropriately?

| Band | Descriptor (condensed) |
|---|---|
| 9 | Fully addresses all parts. Clear position throughout. Fully extended and well-supported ideas. |
| 8 | Sufficiently addresses all parts. Clear, well-developed position. Relevant, extended, well-supported ideas. |
| 7 | Addresses all parts, some more fully than others. Clear position. Main ideas extended but may overgeneralize or lack focus. |
| 6 | Addresses the task, but some parts more fully covered. Position relevant but conclusions may be unclear or repetitive. Main ideas relevant but may lack detail. |
| 5 | Addresses the task only partially. Format may be inappropriate. Position expressed but unclear. Limited ideas, inadequately developed. |
| 4 | Minimally addresses task, format inappropriate. Difficult to identify position. Few ideas, largely undeveloped or irrelevant. |
| 3 | Does not adequately address any part. No clear position. Very few ideas, largely undeveloped. |

**Hard caps to watch for:**
- Task 1: No overview sentence → TA capped at 5.
- Task 2: Only partially addresses the question (e.g. discusses one view when both required) → TR capped at 5.
- Under word count (150 Task 1 / 250 Task 2) → penalty applied.

---

## 2. Coherence and Cohesion (25%)

How logically is the response organized, and how well do ideas link?

| Band | Descriptor (condensed) |
|---|---|
| 9 | Skillful cohesion that attracts no attention. Skillful paragraphing. |
| 8 | Information sequenced logically. All aspects of cohesion well-managed. Uses paragraphing sufficiently and appropriately. |
| 7 | Logically organizes info with clear progression. Uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately (some over/under-use). Clear central topic per paragraph. |
| 6 | Arranges info coherently with overall progression. Uses cohesive devices effectively, but within/between sentences may be faulty or mechanical. May not always use referencing clearly. Uses paragraphing but not always logically. |
| 5 | Presents info with some organization, but lacks overall progression. Inadequate, inaccurate, or overused cohesive devices. May repeat reference. May not write in paragraphs or paragraphing is confusing. |
| 4 | Info presented but not coherently arranged, no clear progression. Basic cohesive devices used inaccurately or repetitively. May not write in paragraphs. |

**Common issues:**
- Overuse of "Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly, Finally" = mechanical, caps at band 6.
- Missing topic sentences = caps at band 6.
- One-sentence "paragraphs" = caps at band 5.

---

## 3. Lexical Resource (25%)

How accurate, flexible, and precise is the vocabulary?

| Band | Descriptor (condensed) |
|---|---|
| 9 | Wide range of vocab used with very natural and sophisticated control. Rare minor errors only as "slips". |
| 8 | Wide range fluently and flexibly. Skillfully uses uncommon lexical items but with occasional inaccuracies in word choice/collocation. Rare errors in spelling/word formation. |
| 7 | Sufficient range to allow some flexibility and precision. Uses less common lexis with awareness of style and collocation. Some errors in word choice, spelling, or word formation. |
| 6 | Adequate range for the task. Some attempts at less common vocab with some inaccuracy. Errors in spelling/word formation don't impede communication. |
| 5 | Limited range, minimally adequate for the task. Noticeable errors in spelling/word formation may cause some difficulty for the reader. |
| 4 | Basic vocab, may be used repetitively or inappropriately. Limited control of word formation/spelling. Errors may cause strain. |

**Red flags:**
- Memorized phrases ("In this ever-changing world", "It is a double-edged sword") → cap at 5-6 on LR.
- Consistent collocation errors ("make a research", "do a mistake") → cap at 6.
- Wrong register (contractions, slang in academic writing) → cap at 6.

---

## 4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (25%)

How varied and accurate is the grammar?

| Band | Descriptor (condensed) |
|---|---|
| 9 | Wide range of structures with full flexibility and accuracy. Rare minor errors only as "slips". |
| 8 | Wide range. Majority of sentences error-free. Occasional non-systematic errors. |
| 7 | Variety of complex structures. Frequent error-free sentences. Good control of grammar; few errors. |
| 6 | Mix of simple and complex sentence forms. Some errors in grammar and punctuation, but rarely reduce communication. |
| 5 | Limited range of structures. Attempts complex sentences but tend to be less accurate than simple ones. Frequent errors; may cause difficulty for reader. |
| 4 | Very limited range. Some structures accurate but errors predominate. Punctuation often faulty. |

**Key distinctions:**
- **Range** = can the learner use conditionals, relative clauses, passive voice, complex noun phrases?
- **Accuracy** = how often are those structures error-free?
- Band 7 = "frequent error-free sentences" (not all — but a clear majority).
- Band 6 = complex sentences attempted but with errors; errors don't usually block meaning.

---

## Scoring shortcut

If you're unsure between two bands, apply the "best fit" rule: pick the band whose descriptors match the response in the **majority** of criteria. Don't require every element of the descriptor to be met — IELTS grading is holistic.
