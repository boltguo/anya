# Briefing Band Ladder

Adapt length / sentence style / vocab load / audio speed to the learner's current Overall band.

| Band | Article | Sentence style | Vocab load | Audio speed | Support |
| ---- | ------- | -------------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------- |
| `< 5.0` | 180-260 words | short, direct | 3 core words | `-15%` | high |
| `5.0-6.0` | 260-380 words | mixed length | 4-5 target words | `-5%` | medium |
| `6.5+` | 380-550 words | natural, varied | 5-7 target words | `0%` | low |

## Weak-dim overlays

- **Listening weak** → cleaner structure, fewer names/numbers, slower audio, key-points preview
- **Reading weak** → shorter paragraphs, explicit transitions, one-line summary per section
- **Speaking weak** → add 2 discussion prompts after the article
- **Writing weak** → add 2 reusable phrases and 1 short opinion prompt

## Content picker

- Prefer IELTS-useful topics: education / technology / work & society / environment / health & lifestyle / culture & media.
- Respect `Content Profile.avoid`; emotionally heavy stories only if interests clearly support.
- If no fresh topic → evergreen explainer aligned with interests + level.
- Don't repeat consecutive similar briefings.
