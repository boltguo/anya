# Raw Score → Band Conversion

Approximate conversion tables for Reading and Listening. Used by any skill that reports a band estimate from a raw /40 score.

**Disclaimer**: These thresholds are *approximate* and based on publicly available Cambridge IELTS practice test conversions. Real test-day conversions vary slightly by paper. Always caveat estimates as such.

## Overall band calculation

The overall IELTS band is the **arithmetic mean** of the four section bands, rounded to the nearest 0.5. A score ending in 0.25 rounds up to 0.5; a score ending in 0.75 rounds up to the next whole number.

**Examples:**

| L   | R   | W   | S   | Mean | Overall |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|------|---------|
| 7.0 | 6.5 | 6.0 | 6.5 | 6.5  | 6.5     |
| 7.5 | 7.0 | 6.5 | 7.0 | 7.0  | 7.0     |
| 7.0 | 7.0 | 6.5 | 6.5 | 6.75 | 7.0     |
| 6.5 | 6.5 | 6.0 | 6.0 | 6.25 | 6.5     |
| 6.0 | 5.5 | 5.5 | 5.5 | 5.625| 5.5     |


## Usage

Look up the tables above to convert raw scores. For overall band, compute the arithmetic mean of 4 sections and round to nearest 0.5 (0.25 → 0.5, 0.75 → next whole).
